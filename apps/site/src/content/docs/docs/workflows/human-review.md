---
title: Human review workflow
description: Keep accountable acceptance separate from execution and automated verification.
---

Human review follows independent verification.

The reviewer receives:

- the requested outcome and requirement map;
- changed source and decisions;
- test and discrimination evidence;
- policy exceptions or approvals;
- unresolved risks and rollback information;
- package, source, and report digests.

The reviewer can accept, reject, or request changes. Acceptance is signed and tied to the exact evidence set. A later source or evidence change invalidates the decision instead of silently carrying it forward.

Automation may recommend a decision. It cannot become the accountable human.
