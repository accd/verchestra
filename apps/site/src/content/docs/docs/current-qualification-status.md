The public version is `0.0.0-qualification`.

## Verified now

Foundations through T68b have public validation reports. They cover the CLI, workspace placement, policy, drivers, probes, evidence, memory, distribution, recovery, human-review contracts, the persistent signing-key lifecycle, and enforced cost and duration ceilings.

## Next

T68c and T68d finish hardening the existing foundations: declarative gate repair and the policy boundary. T69 then builds the isolated Self-Test trust domain, and T70–T77 add profiles, deep diagnostics, regression and holdout evaluation, platform qualification, reproducible release artifacts, and the explicit 1.0 decision.

:::caution[No implied release]
Completed foundations are evidence about implemented contracts. They do not mean Verchestra is installable, production-ready, or accepted as 1.0.
:::

Read the canonical [roadmap](/verchestra/roadmap/) or inspect the [qualification evidence](/verchestra/docs/qualification/).
